I am currently an AI/ML Research Specialist at the Samsung Research Institute Brazil (SRBR), where I contribute to the development of cutting-edge solutions in signal and image processing.
My work spans a range of advanced AI-driven projects, including health, fitness, and biometric applications, where I leverage deep learning, machine learning, and data-driven techniques to drive meaningful innovation.
With a strong emphasis on research and development, I am committed to advancing AI solutions that push the boundaries of technology in real-world applications, enhancing both accuracy and efficiency across various domains.

I earned my M.Sc. degree in Computer Science from the Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG) in 2017, where I focused on advancing my expertise in artificial intelligence and machine learning.
Currently, I am pursuing a Ph.D. at UFMG under the guidance of Prof. Dr. William Robson Schwartz, a respected leader in the field of computer vision and biometrics.
My doctoral research delves into innovative methodologies in AI, particularly within biometric recognition and health-related applications, aiming to contribute meaningful advancements in these fields.
This academic journey allows me to deepen my theoretical foundation while also applying research in real-world scenarios.
